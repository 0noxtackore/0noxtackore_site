## What 0NOXTACKORE is

0NOXTACKORE is an independent technology company founded in 2023 by Angello Aponte. It focuses on software engineering, open-source development, integrations, technical consulting, and curated technology recommendations.

0NOXTACKORE is **not** a game top-up store, gaming recharge site, or gaming marketplace.

## Areas of activity

- **Software development** — Applications and tools for real everyday, technical, and professional problems.
- **Open-source engineering** — Adapting, improving, and distributing open-source technologies with added value while respecting licenses and authors.
- **Technologies and integrations** — Tools, SDKs, systems, and integrations for developers and companies.
- **Technology products** — Software and sustainable digital solutions with clear purpose.
- **Technology recommendation and affiliation** — Independent product curation based on technical criteria and value for money. Not a manufacturer or direct seller.
- **Technical consulting and support** — Architecture, implementation, and evolution support.
